Privacy Notice

Version 2026-11-05d · Last updated 5 November 2026

In short: a school’s records (pupils, parents, staff, fees) belong to the school. We keep them safe and use them only to run Zetclass for that school. We do not sell personal information and we do not show advertising. We follow Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

1. Who is responsible for what

  • The school decides what information about its pupils, parents and staff goes into Zetclass and why. For that information, the school is the data controller.
  • Zetranova Limited (“we”) runs Zetclass and handles the school’s information on the school’s behalf, to provide the service (we are a data processor). For the school’s own sign-up and billing details, we are the controller.

2. What we collect

  • When a school signs up (required): school name, country, email address, phone number for calls (with country code), and a password. The password is never stored in readable form.
  • When a school signs up (optional): a separate WhatsApp number, website, region, district, locality / area and postal address. You can leave these empty, and add, change or remove them later.
  • What the school adds: pupils’ names, classes and admission details; parents’ or guardians’ names and phone numbers; attendance; exam scores and report remarks; fee bills and payments; staff names, roles and pay details; stock and accounting records; messages and announcements. Which of these a school uses is the school’s choice.
  • Billing: the plan, the dates, and the Mobile Money transaction details a school sends us when it pays.
  • Proof of authority: when a school registers we record that the person confirmed they are the owner or an authorised representative, with the time and the connection (IP) address. If we run an ownership check we also keep the school’s licence or registration number you give us and a short note of how we checked it.
  • Technical and safety records: sign-in attempts (to stop guessing), and a private log of actions our team takes on a school’s account (for example extending a trial or resetting a password).

3. Why we use it

  • To create the school’s account, run Zetclass for it and give support (performance of our agreement with the school).
  • To bill, keep accounts and meet our legal duties.
  • To keep Zetclass secure and prevent misuse (our legitimate interest).
  • To contact the Administrator about the account, such as trial reminders or password help.

We do not use pupil information for advertising, profiling or any purpose unrelated to running the school’s Zetclass.

4. Children’s information

Pupil records are entered and controlled by the school. We never contact pupils. Schools should tell parents and guardians how pupil information is used and get any consent the law requires.

5. Who else sees it

  • Our hosting provider stores Zetclass and its backups on servers that may be outside Ghana. We use a reputable provider and share only what is needed to run the service.
  • Our email sending (for example password-reset emails) goes through our hosting’s mail system.
  • The authorities, only where the law requires it.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • Within a school, what each person can see depends on their role (Administrator, Director, Teacher, Other staff, Parent). One school can never see another school’s records.

6. How we protect it

  • The connection is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored only as one-way scrambled values, never in readable form.
  • Each school’s records are kept separate, and the server checks every request.
  • Roles limit what each person can see and change. Our own access for support is logged.
  • We keep backups and ask schools to keep their own downloaded copies too.

No system is perfectly secure. If a security problem affects personal information, we will tell the school and, where the Data Protection Act requires, the Data Protection Commission and the people affected.

7. How long we keep it

  • While a school’s subscription or trial is active.
  • After it ends, the school’s records are kept view-only for 12 months so the school can download them, and may then be deleted. We remind the school before deleting.
  • Billing records are kept as long as accounting and tax rules require.
  • A school can ask us to delete its school’s records sooner (see section 9).

8. Browser storage

While you are signed in, Zetclass keeps a sign-in token in your browser tab so you do not have to type your password on every page. Closing the tab ends it. The app also saves copies of its own pages and pictures on your device so it loads faster and works with a poor connection. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you can ask to see the personal information held about you, to have mistakes corrected, to object to certain uses, and to have it deleted where the law allows. For information about a pupil, parent or staff member, please ask the school first, because the school is responsible for that record. For anything else, or if the school cannot help, contact us. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

10. Changes

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows the latest version. For important changes we will tell schools in Zetclass or by WhatsApp.
