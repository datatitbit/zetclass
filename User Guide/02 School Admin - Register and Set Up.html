Creating your school's account on Zetclass for the first time.
Free trial
The first 30 days are free. No payment details are needed.

You. The account that can add people, change settings and manage the school.
Portal address
Your school's own link, e.g. zetclass.com/st-marys. You choose it when you register.
Region · District · Locality
Where the school is: the region (e.g. Ashanti), the district or municipality (e.g. Kumasi) and the town or area (e.g. Adum).

The number on the school's official licence or registration, e.g. from NaSIA or the Ghana Education Service.
Verified
A mark added by Zetranova once it has checked that you really own or represent the school.
Access Control
The Settings page where you add and manage logins for staff and parents.
Sample data
Pretend pupils, fees and records so you can explore. You can remove them in one click.
Read-only
You can look at everything but not change anything. This happens if the trial or subscription ends. Your data stays safe.

Part 1 · Register your school (first time)

Before you startOnly the owner or an authorised representative may register a school. Registering a school you are not allowed to represent is a false claim and can make you personally liable. Zetclass records your confirmation.

Have ready: the school's full name; its email address (reset links go here); a phone number for calls; the region, district and town; and a password of at least 8 characters.

  1. Go to zetclass.com and tap Sign up and start your free 30-day trial. [On a phone it is also the Sign up link in the top bar.] Or go straight to zetclass.com/signup.
  2. Your school · type the School name in full. Choose your Country (Ghana is already selected).
  3. How we can reach you · type the School email address and the School phone number (for calls). Type only the main number, e.g. 24 123 4567: the country code (+233) is added for you. A WhatsApp number is optional, only if it is different from the phone.
  4. Where is the school? · choose the Region, then the District / municipality (the list changes to match your region), then type the Locality / area.
    • District not listed? Choose Other (not listed) at the bottom of the list.
    • Outside Ghana? Type your region, state or province instead.
  5. Your school’s portal address · this becomes your school’s link, e.g. zetclass.com/st-marys. Zetclass suggests three from your school name: the main name (without words like Primary or JHS), the full name with no hyphens, and the main name plus details (e.g. achimota-jhs). Tap one, or type your own. It must include part of your school’s name or its initials. A green ✓ Available means it is free. Then tick I agree that … is added to zetclass.com as my school’s portal address.
    • Choose carefully: it is used on sign-in links, notices and QR codes, and cannot be changed later without contacting Zetranova.
  6. Secure your account · type a password. The coloured bar shows how strong it is. It is only advice: you can still register with a weak password, but a strong one protects your school.
  7. Optional details · add the school's Website and Postal address if you like, or skip them.
  8. Before you start · leave Start with sample data ticked if you want to explore with pretend records. Tick I am the owner or an authorised representative… and tick I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Notice. Both are required.
  9. Tap Create my school.
CheckA page says Your school is ready and shows your school's portal address, your username (admin) and your free-trial end date. Tap Copy these details and keep them safe, then tap Open my school.
Good to knowEach school name, email address and phone number can be registered once. If "St. Mary's Basic School" is taken, add your town, e.g. "St. Mary's Basic School, Kumasi". Required boxes are marked with a red * and the word Required; the others say Optional.

Part 2 · Set up the school profile

  1. Sign in (see Guide 1). Choose user type Admin, username admin.
  2. Open Settings [left menu] and the Profile tab.
  3. Check the school name, address, country, region, district, locality, phone, WhatsApp, email and website. Add the logo, signature and stamp images if you have them.
  4. Tap Save changes.
  5. Visit the Academic tab (year and term) and Classes & Subjects to match your school.

Part 3 · Verify that you own the school

Verification protects real schools from people who pretend to be them. Zetranova checks your licence number and may phone you.

  1. Open the Subscription page [tap Manage subscription in the banner at the top, or use the left menu].
  2. In the Verify your school card, type your Licence / registration number.
  3. Tap Submit for checking. You can also tap Tell us on WhatsApp to send it.
CheckThe card says "We have your number…". When Zetranova has checked it, the card changes to a green Verified ✓. If you change the number later, the Verified mark is removed until it is checked again.

Part 4 · Add, rename and remove staff and parent accounts

ImportantOnly the school Admin can add, rename or remove accounts and reset other people's passwords. Everyone else can only change their own password.

Add an account

  1. Open Settings → the Access Control tab.
  2. In Login accounts, tap + Add login account.
  3. Type the person's Full name. Choose their User type: Teaching staff, Non-teaching staff, Admin, Director or Parent / guardian.
  4. Optional: use Link to staff/parent record to connect the login to their record.
  5. Type a Username (letters, numbers, dots, dashes or underscores, no spaces, e.g. k.mensah). Each username must be different from everyone else's at your school.
  6. Type an Initial password (at least 8 characters). Tap Create.
  7. Tell the person four things: school name, user type, username, password, and that they sign in at zetclass.com. They are asked to choose their own password at first sign-in.

Rename a username, reset a password, or remove an account

In the same Login accounts table, each row has three buttons:

  • Rename username · type the new username and save. Tell the person: their password stays the same.
  • Reset password · type a new password twice and save. They must change it when they next sign in.
  • Remove · confirm. They can no longer sign in. Records they created (pupils, scores, payments) are not deleted.
Rules that protect youUsernames are never duplicated (capital letters are ignored). You cannot remove the account you are signed in with, and the school must always keep at least one Admin.
TipGive staff their school's own link (zetclass.com/your-school) so the school name is already filled in when they sign in.

Part 5 · Subscribe after the free trial

  1. Open the Subscription page.
  2. Look at the Plans table and pick Basic, Growth or Premium by the number of pupils.
  3. In How to subscribe, tap Message us on WhatsApp. The message is written for you with your school's details.
  4. Pay by Mobile Money to the MoMo number and Merchant ID shown on that page.
  5. Send the transaction ID on WhatsApp. Zetranova confirms your payment.
CheckThe Subscription page shows Active and a new Paid until date. If the trial or subscription ends, the school becomes read-only (everything visible, nothing editable) until you renew. Your data is not deleted.

Part 6 · Back up your data

  1. Open Settings → the Data tab.
  2. Tap Export data (JSON). A file downloads to your device.
  3. Keep the file somewhere safe, such as your email or a flash drive. Do this at least once a month, and before any big change.
CarefulImport data and Reset on the same page replace the school's records. Use them only if you are sure.

Part 7 · Sample data

If you kept Start with sample data ticked, you have pretend pupils, fees, attendance, scores, stock and accounts. When you are ready for real records:

  1. Open Settings → the Sample Data tab. [Or use the banner on the dashboard.]
  2. Tick the groups to remove and tap the remove button. Confirm.

Records you added yourself are not touched. You can add the sample groups back any time from the same tab.

Part 8 · Register students and staff

There are two ways. Use whichever is easier.

Way 1 · Direct entry

  1. Students: open Students and tap + Admit student. Staff: open Administration → staff and tap + Add staff.
  2. Fill in the form. Boxes marked * are required. Tap Save.

Way 2 · Download a template, fill it, upload it

  1. On Students, tap ⤓ New-admissions template. (For staff: ⤓ New-staff template on the staff page.) A spreadsheet file downloads.
  2. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets. The first row shows the column names; a * means required. The next two rows start with #GUIDE and #EXAMPLE and show what to type. Delete those two rows (they are skipped anyway).
  3. Type one student per row. Dates look like 2018-05-01 (day/month/year such as 05/03/2018 also works). For choices, type one of the listed options. For Yes/No boxes type Yes or No.
  4. Save the file as CSV. Tap ⤒ Upload new admissions and choose the file.
  5. Zetclass shows how many rows are valid and lists any problem rows with the line number and reason. Tap Import to add the valid ones. Fix the rest in your file and upload again.
The template always matches your formThe template is built from your Admission Form (Settings → Admission Form). If you add, rename or remove a field there (for example "Parent occupation type"), download a fresh template and the new column is there. An older template still works: columns it does not have simply stay empty, and Zetclass tells you which ones.
To correct students already on rollUse Update existing student records on the Students page: download the data template for a class or one student (it comes pre-filled), change what you need, keep student_id unchanged, and upload it back. Only the columns in your file are changed.
NoteStaff forms have fixed fields today, so the staff template does not change when you edit the student Admission Form.

Tips

  • Use a strong password and do not share the Admin login. Create separate accounts for other people.
  • Keep the school's registered email working: password reset links are sent there.
  • Write down the username and password for each new account only on paper you keep safe, and ask people to change their password at first sign-in.
