Everyday sign-in

For teaching staff, non-teaching staff, Admin, Director and Parents.

Who: every Zetclass userLast updated: 5 Nov 2026App version: 20261125← All guides

Before you start

Words in this section
School name
The full name of your school.
User type
The group you belong to: Teaching staff, Non-teaching staff, Admin, Director or Parent.
Username
The name you type to sign in. Your school Admin gives it to you.
  • Ask your school Admin for your username and your first password.
  • Use any phone, tablet or computer with internet. No app to install.
  • Check that you know which user type you are. Your Admin can tell you.

Sign in

Words in this section
School link
Your school’s own web link, like zetclass.com/st-marys. It already knows your school.
Suggestion list
The list of school names that appears while you type. Tap yours.
Password
Your secret word. Tap Show to see what you typed.
  1. Open your browser and go to zetclass.com.
    • Shortcut: your school may give you its own link, such as zetclass.com/st-marys. Then the school is already known: you do not type the school name (go straight to step 3). Tap Choose a different school if it is the wrong one.
  2. In School name, start typing the full name of your school. A list appears. Pick your school from the list.
  3. In User type, open the menu and choose: Teaching staff, Non-teaching staff, Admin, Director or Parent / guardian. A short note under the box explains each one.
  4. In Username, type the username your Admin gave you.
  5. In Password, type your password. Tap Show if you want to see what you typed.
  6. Tap Sign in.
CheckYou see the Zetclass dashboard, and your name and user type at the top right.
TipThe next time you open Zetclass on the same phone or computer, your school name and user type are remembered. You only type your username and password.

Your first sign-in: choose a new password

Words in this section
Temporary password
A first password your Admin gave you. You change it the first time you sign in.
Strong password
A long password with capital letters, numbers and a symbol.

If your Admin gave you a temporary password, Zetclass asks you to set your own.

  1. Type a new password in New password. It must have at least 8 characters.
  2. Type it again in Confirm, then save.
Tip — a good passwordUse 10 or more characters with a capital letter, a number and a symbol such as ! or #. Do not use your name, phone number or "password".

Change your password later

Words in this section
Account menu
The menu you open by tapping your name at the top right.
  1. Tap your name and user type at the top right [name ▾].
  2. Tap Change password.
  3. Enter your current password, then the new one, and save.

You can change your own password at any time. You cannot change your own username: only your school Admin can rename it.

Log out

Words in this section
Log out
Signing out, so nobody else can use your account.
  1. Tap your name at the top right.
  2. Tap Log out.
ImportantAlways log out on a shared or public computer.

Working without internet (offline)

Words in this section
Offline
No internet, or a very weak one.
Saved on this device
Your work is kept safely on your phone or computer.
Sync
Zetclass sends the saved work to the server when the internet is back.
Keep me signed in
A tick box so the app opens again later, even without internet.

Zetclass keeps working when the network is slow or off. Whatever you enter is saved on your device and synced automatically when the network comes back. This covers pupil and staff details, new registrations, scores, attendance, fees and payments, and stock.

  1. Sign in once while you have internet, and tick Keep me signed in on this device if it is your own phone or computer. Open the pages you need once so Zetclass can save them.
  2. If the network drops, carry on working. A small message at the bottom says Working offline.
  3. When the network returns, Zetclass sends your work by itself. The message says Synced ✓ and your screens refresh.
Good to know
  • New ID numbers (such as ST0042) are set aside for your device in small batches, so two phones never use the same number.
  • Logging out clears what Zetclass saved on the device. If something has not synced yet, Zetclass syncs it first, or warns you before it would be lost.
  • Signing in, school settings, login accounts and text messages need internet.
  • If you stay signed in, you are signed out after 12 hours.
Shared computersOnly tick Keep me signed in on your own device. Always tap Log out on a shared one.

Install Zetclass as an app

Words in this section
Install
Put Zetclass on your phone or computer like an app, with its own icon.
Home screen
The screen with all your app icons.
  1. On the sign-in page tap ⤓ Install Zetclass on this device. After signing in you can also find Install app on this device in the menu at the top right.
  2. On an iPhone, tap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.

If you forget your password

Words in this section
Reset
Getting a new password.
Registered email
The school email address given when the school joined Zetclass.
  • Teaching and non-teaching staff, Director, Parents: ask your school Admin. They set a new password for you in Settings → Access Control → Reset password. Then sign in and choose your own password.
  • School Admin: on the sign-in page tap Forgot password?. A reset link is emailed to the school's registered email address. If it does not arrive (check Spam too), contact Zetranova on WhatsApp.

If it does not work

Words in this section
Disabled account
Your Admin has switched your sign-in off.
Subscription
The school’s payment for Zetclass. If it has ended, the school can only look, not change.
Caps Lock
A keyboard key that makes every letter a capital. Passwords care about capitals.
You seeLikely causeWhat to do
"We could not sign you in…"One of: school name, user type, username or password is wrong.Check each box. Make sure the user type is the one your Admin set for you. Check Caps Lock.
"Too many sign-in attempts"Several wrong tries in a row.Wait 15 minutes, then try again carefully.
"This account has been disabled"Your Admin switched your login off.Ask your school Admin.
"…subscription is inactive"The school's subscription has ended or been paused.Tell your school Admin. They can renew from the Subscription page.
Your school does not appear in the listSpelling, or the school is not registered.Type more of the name, or use your school's own link.
Tip — add to your phone's home screenIn your phone browser's menu, choose "Add to Home screen". Zetclass then opens like an app. (The exact menu wording depends on your phone.)
