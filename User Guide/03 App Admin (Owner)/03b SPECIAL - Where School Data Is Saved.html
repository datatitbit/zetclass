Where school data is saved

What is stored, where it lives, how schools are kept apart, and how to back it up.

Who: the Zetclass owner (Zetranova) onlyLast updated: 5 Nov 2026App version: 20261118← App Admin guideAll guides

Words to know
Database
An organised digital filing cabinet on a server. Zetclass uses one called MySQL.
Server / hosting
A computer that is always on and connected to the internet. Yours is rented from Namecheap.
cPanel
The hosting control panel where you manage files, databases and email.
API
The "receptionist": the part of Zetclass that fetches and saves records for the app.
School id
The short name that labels one school's records, e.g. st-marys-basic-school. Also the end of its portal address.
Table
One drawer in the filing cabinet, e.g. the list of schools.
Hash
A password scrambled one way, so it can be checked but never read back.
Token
A signed pass the browser carries after sign-in. It names the person, their school and their user type.
Backup
A copy kept somewhere else, so you can recover from a mistake or failure.
Data processor / controller
Under Ghana's Data Protection Act: the processor handles data for someone else (Zetranova for a school's pupil records); the controller decides why and how (Zetranova for sign-up and billing details).

The short answer

School data is not kept on teachers' phones or computers. It is saved in one MySQL database on your web hosting account, and every school's records carry that school's id so they stay apart. Your phone or computer only holds a sign-in pass and the app's own files.

A simple picture

Imagine one large filing room. Every school has its own locked drawer, labelled with its school id. When someone signs in, the receptionist (the API) hands them a pass that names one drawer. From then on, the receptionist only ever opens that drawer for them. Nobody can ask for another school's drawer, even by trying.

Where exactly

What is inside the database

Table (drawer)What it holds
schoolsOne row per school: name, portal address, plan, trial and paid dates, status, contact details, country/region/district/locality, licence number and Verified mark, your private notes.
documentsThe school's day-to-day records, each tagged with the school id: pupils, parents, staff, login accounts, classes, attendance, scores, fees and payments, stock, accounts and more.
singletonsOne-of-a-kind items per school: the school profile (including its logo, signature and stamp images), academic year and term, grading and report settings, access settings.
meta_seqCounters that make the next pupil or staff number (e.g. ST0042).
platform_auditThe activity log of everything the owner does, and each signup.
password_resetsOne-hour reset links and "Forgot password" requests (the link is stored scrambled).
rate_limitsCounters that slow down guessing (sign-in tries, signups).
app_metaThe database's version number, so updates upgrade it safely.

How schools are kept apart

  • Every record carries its school id, and every request is filtered by the school id inside the sign-in pass, never by anything the browser sends.
  • The pass is signed with a secret only the server knows, and expires after about 12 hours.
  • A school Admin can only touch their own school. The owner's control room is a separate account.
  • Tests confirm that one school cannot read or change another's records.

How passwords are stored

  • Passwords are stored only as hashes (PBKDF2, 100,000 rounds, with a random salt). Nobody, including you, can read them back.
  • Temporary passwords are shown once on screen. Zetclass does not keep a readable copy.
  • Staff passwords are scrambled in the person's browser before they are sent.

Backups: what you have, and what to do

What we found (checked in your cPanel, 5 Nov 2026)
  • Namecheap does not show you any automatic backups. The Backup page says none are available unless the server owner turns them on, and there is no JetBackup tool.
  • You can download backups by hand. Your Zetclass database is named zetrekzh_zetclass and is tiny (about 1.7 MB), so a backup takes seconds.
  • So your own downloads are your only safety net. Do not rely on Namecheap having a copy.

Where to keep the copies (you only have Namecheap: that is fine)

A backup must live somewhere other than the hosting account, otherwise one problem can lose both. You already have two places, and no new account is needed:

  • Your computer's OneDrive (the Zetranova Product folder already lives there). Make a folder called Zetclass Backups. This is the main place.
  • Your Gmail / Google Drive (optional second copy). Upload the same file to Google Drive, or email it to yourself.
Keep them privateA backup contains every school's pupil, parent and staff information. Keep the folder private: do not share it, and do not send it to anyone.

Take a backup (about 2 minutes, once a week)

  1. Sign in to Namecheap, open Hosting List, and next to your hosting plan choose Go to cPanel. [You reach the same cPanel you already use.]
  2. In cPanel, under Files, open Backup.
  3. Scroll to Partial Backups. Under Download a Database Backup, click zetrekzh_zetclass.
  4. A database file downloads (usually ending in .sql.gz). Open your Downloads folder and rename it with today's date, e.g. zetclass-db-2026-11-05.sql.gz.
  5. Move it into the OneDrive folder Zetclass Backups.
CheckThe file is in OneDrive, shows a green tick (synced), and is larger than 0 KB.

Once, and after big changes: also click Download a Full Account Backup [top of the same page]. It includes the private settings file (config.local.php) that the database backup does not. cPanel prepares it, which can take a few minutes, and tells you when it is ready to download (it may send an email). (The exact wording on that page was not checked.)

TipKeep the last 8 weekly files, and delete older ones. They are small. Put a repeating reminder on your phone for the same day each week.

Test a restore once

A backup you have never tried is only a hope. Ask your developer or assistant to restore one into a test database and check that a school opens. This guide will gain exact restore steps once that has been done.

Also good to know

  • Each school can export its own data. The school Admin: Settings → Data → Export data (JSON). Encourage schools to do this monthly. It is their own copy, and it does not replace yours.
  • The code is safe on GitHub, so you do not need to back up the program files, only the database and the private settings file.
  • Optional next step: an automatic nightly copy kept on the server (using a cPanel scheduled task). It protects against mistakes such as an accidental delete, but not against losing the hosting account, so it adds to your weekly download and does not replace it. Ask your developer or assistant to set it up if you want it.

What happens when a school is deleted or reset

  • Delete school permanently removes the school's row, all its records (documents), its settings (singletons), its counters and its reset links. The activity log keeps a line that the school was deleted (its name and when), not its pupil records.
  • Reset school data removes records, settings and counters but keeps the school's row (subscription, contact details).
  • Deleted data cannot be recovered except from a backup you took earlier.

Looking ahead

The plan is to keep the MySQL database on shared hosting for now. As Zetclass grows, the data can move to a larger database (e.g. PostgreSQL) on a dedicated server. When that happens, this page will be updated and the update log below will say so.

Checklist for the owner

  • ☐ Keep the cPanel and control-room passwords private; turn on two-step sign-in for hosting.
  • ☐ Download the database backup every week and keep it in OneDrive (and optionally Google Drive).
  • ☐ Remember: Namecheap shows you no automatic backups, so your downloads are the only copy.
  • ☐ Register with the Ghana Data Protection Commission and keep the Privacy Notice up to date.
  • ☐ Delete your own test schools after trying signup.
