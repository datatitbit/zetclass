Words to know
Control room
The owner's private screen at zetclass.com/admin. Also called the platform dashboard.
Platform account
The owner's login. It is separate from every school's logins.
School (tenant)
One customer school. Each has its own private space.
Manage
The button that opens everything about one school.
Free trial
30 days from signup, free. You can extend it.
Paid until
The date a paid subscription runs to. After it, the school is read-only.
Verified
Your mark that a school's ownership has been checked.
Temporary password
A one-time password you give a school. They must change it at first sign-in.
Log in as this school
Opens that school's account as its Admin, for support. It is time-limited and recorded.
Suspend
Pause a school: it becomes view-only. Reversible.
Reset / Delete
Reset wipes a school's records but keeps its account. Delete removes the school completely. Delete cannot be undone.
Activity log
A permanent record of everything done in the control room.

Part 1 · Sign in to the control room

  1. Go to zetclass.com/admin. [Type exactly admin: there is no .com/admi.]
  2. In Platform username, type the owner username.
  3. In Password, type the owner password. Tap Sign in.
CheckYou see Zetranova Platform at the top and a Schools page with count tiles.
Where the login is keptThe owner username and password are not stored in the app's records. They are kept (as a scrambled value) in a private settings file on the hosting server. To change them, ask your developer or assistant: do not post them in chats.

To sign out: tap your name [Platform ▾] at the top right and choose Log out.

Part 2 · Tour of the Schools screen

  • Tiles at the top count your schools: Schools, On free trial, Paid, Ending ≤ 7 days, Ended, Suspended. Tap a tile to filter the list.
  • Search box finds a school by name, email, phone or portal address. The menu next to it filters the same way as the tiles (plus New this week).
  • Table columns: School, Contact, Subscription, Plan, and a Manage button.
  • Tags under a school's name: New (joined this week), Verified (ownership checked), Check licence (the school gave a licence number, waiting for you), Not verified (no number yet).
  • Password help requests card (appears when needed): Admins who pressed "Forgot password". See Part 5.
  • Activity log and + Add school buttons at the top right.

Part 3 · Check a school's ownership (before taking payment)

  1. Tap Manage next to the school. The first section is Ownership check.
  2. Read the Licence / registration number given by the school.
  3. Look it up with the issuing body, e.g. NaSIA for private schools or GES for public ones.
  4. Call or WhatsApp the school's phone number on file (shown under Contact & notes). Confirm the person really is the owner or an authorised representative.
  5. In the box, write how you checked, e.g. NaSIA no. confirmed; called owner 5 Nov.
  6. Tap Mark as Verified.
CheckThe section shows a green Verified with the date and your note. The school's own Subscription page now shows Verified ✓. To undo, tap Remove Verified mark and give a reason (recorded in the activity log).
Why this mattersAnyone can type a school name into the sign-up form. Verification stops an impostor from running a school's account or paying in its name. While a school is not Verified, the Subscription section shows a warning above Record a payment.

Part 4 · Record a payment and manage the free trial

When a school has paid

  1. The school sends its MoMo transaction ID on WhatsApp. Confirm the money arrived in your MoMo account.
  2. Tap Manage next to the school → Subscription section.
  3. Under Record a payment, choose the Plan (Basic, Growth or Premium) and the period: 1 term, 2 terms, Full year, or Pick an end date….
  4. Optional: type the amount and a note such as the MoMo reference.
  5. Read the line that says "School will be paid until …". Tap Record payment.
CheckA message says "Payment recorded". The school shows Paid with its new date. Renewals add to any time already paid.

Extend a free trial

In the same Subscription section, under Free trial, tap + 1 month, + 2 weeks or + 3 months; or type a number of days and tap Add days; or choose an exact date and tap Set date.

CarefulExtending a trial on a school that has already paid replaces its paid date. The screen asks you to confirm first.

Part 5 · Help a school

A school Admin forgot their password

  1. Look at Password help requests on the Schools page. It lists Admins who pressed "Forgot password" and whether their email was sent.
  2. Confirm it really is the school: tap WhatsApp them or call the number on file.
  3. Tap Issue temporary password and confirm. A box shows the one-time password and a ready-made WhatsApp message.
  4. Send it to the school. They sign in and choose their own password. Tap Dismiss on the request when done.
Other accountsTeaching and non-teaching staff and parents ask their own school Admin to reset passwords. You only need to help Admins. In Manage → Login accounts you can also reset a password or disable an account for any user.

Correct a school's details

Manage → Contact & notes → Edit school details… lets you change the name, contact person, email, phone, WhatsApp, website, country, region, district, locality and postal address. The portal address (e.g. zetclass.com/st-marys) cannot change. Use Private notes for your own reminders (schools never see them) and tap Save note.

See what a school sees

Manage → Support tools → Log in as this school opens the school as its Admin, for a limited time. The screen shows a banner while you are in. This is recorded in the activity log. Activity for this school shows its history.

Part 6 · Add a school by hand · suspend · reset · delete

Add a school by hand

  1. Tap + Add school [top right].
  2. Fill in School name, Contact person (becomes the first Admin), Email, Phone / WhatsApp and, if you want, a Portal address.
  3. Leave the sample-demo box unticked for a real school. Tap Create.
  4. A box shows the school's temporary password and a WhatsApp message to send. Hand it over: the school must change it at first sign-in.

Suspend, reset, delete

  • Suspend school / Reactivate school (Manage → Support tools): pauses or restores a school. Suspended schools are view-only.
  • Reset school data… (Danger zone): wipes every pupil, staff member, payment and record, keeps the account and subscription, and gives a new temporary password.
  • Delete school permanently… (Danger zone): removes the school and all its data. Use it for test signups, or when a school asks for its information to be erased.
Cannot be undoneReset and Delete cannot be reversed. The screen asks you to confirm by typing. Export the school's data first if there is any chance it is needed (the school Admin can do this from Settings → Data).

Part 7 · The activity log

  1. Tap Activity log [top right of the Schools page] for everything, or Activity for this school inside Manage for one school.
  2. Each line shows the time, what happened (e.g. School signed up, Recorded payment, Marked school as Verified, Issued temporary password) and the details.

Entries cannot be edited or deleted from the app. For signups, the log also shows that the person confirmed they are the owner or authorised representative, and from which internet address.

Tips

  • Check ownership before you record a payment.
  • Delete your own test schools after you try the signup.
  • If a school says it never got the reset email, check Spam, then use the temporary password route.
